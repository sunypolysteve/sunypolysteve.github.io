Why this activity

  • Ong & Eisenstein: media theorists who studied how print (vs. oral/manuscript culture) reshaped thought itself — not just communication. Their ideas explain why format changes cognition, the course's core claim.
  • Phaedrus: Plato's dialogue where Socrates warns writing weakens memory — the first recorded fear of an information technology, and the template for every fear since.
  • Wikipedia: a rigorously human-edited, anti-AI, citation-sourced encyclopedia — your "ground truth" for this assignment, distinct from Claude's output.
  • "Generate with Claude": using AI to build something new from sourced material, not to replace your own thinking about it.
